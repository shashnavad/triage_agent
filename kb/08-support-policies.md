## Support Policies

### How to reach us

- **Help Center** (preferred): help.northwindbooks.example

- **Email**: support@northwindbooks.example

- **In-app chat**: the “?” icon in the bottom-right of the app

Hours: Monday–Saturday, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM Pacific. P0 issues are covered 24/7.

### Priority definitions and response targets

| **Priority** | **Definition** | **First response** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 – Critical | App unavailable, or a security/privacy incident (e.g., data visible to the wrong account) | 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| P1 – High | Core workflow broken or blocked with financial, legal, or hard-deadline impact (e.g., payroll, tax filing); no reasonable workaround | 2 hours |
| P2 – Medium | Feature not working as expected, data issue, or billing question; workaround exists or impact is limited | 1 business day |
| P3 – Low | How-to questions, minor issues, feature requests | 3 business days |

### Things Support can’t do through a ticket

- **Plan or pricing changes and contract terms**: handled by your Account Manager.

- **Subscription billing disputes**: handled by billing@northwindbooks.example. Your subscription is billed on the number of **active team members** on the 1st of each month.

- **Data deletion requests** (e.g., under privacy laws): must be submitted by an account Owner to privacy@northwindbooks.example. Northwind must verify the request and any legal retention requirements before deleting anything. Support can’t delete records directly.

- **Tax, legal, or accounting advice.**

### Feature requests

We log feature requests for our Product team, which reviews them quarterly. Support can’t commit to timelines.

### Security

Never send passwords, API keys, card numbers, or bank account numbers by email or chat. Use the secure upload in the Help Center. Northwind staff will never ask for your password or API key.

If you think you’re seeing another account’s data, **stop using the affected screen and contact us immediately**. We treat this as P0.
