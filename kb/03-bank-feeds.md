## Bank Feeds & Reconciliation

Connect your business bank and card accounts so transactions flow into Northwind Books automatically.

### Connecting a bank

Go to **Banking → Connect account**, search for your bank, and sign in through the secure connection window. We support over 9,000 banks and card issuers.

- After you connect, we import up to **12 months of past transactions**. This usually takes **5–30 minutes**, and up to **24 hours** for large accounts.

- After that, feeds sync automatically **every 4 hours**. You can click **Sync now** at most once per hour.

### Feed statuses

| **Status** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Connected | Syncing normally |
| Syncing | A sync is in progress |
| Needs Reauthorization | Your bank needs you to sign in again. Click **Reconnect**. |
| Delayed | Your bank hasn’t sent new transactions on schedule |
| Disconnected | The connection was removed or failed permanently |

#### “Delayed” for a long time

Some banks send transactions late, especially over weekends and holidays. Up to **3 business days** of delay is normal. If a feed has been **Delayed for more than 3 business days**, open a support ticket with the bank name and account (last 4 digits only). Our team will escalate with the bank’s data provider. We can’t speed up a bank that is still within the normal window.

### Reconciliation

- **Match** pairs a bank transaction with an invoice payment or expense.

- **Match All** auto-matches every suggested pair on the screen.

- If an invoice shows **Unpaid** even though the client paid, check **Banking → Unmatched** for the deposit and match it. If the deposit is already matched to the invoice and the invoice still shows Unpaid after you click **Sync now**, open a support ticket.
